Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 2026. This policy explains how the owner of Jakkur Villa ("we") handles personal data shared through this website, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

When you book a visit, request documents or download the brochure, we collect the details you type in: your name, phone number, email address, preferred date and time, the type of request and any message. We may also record the advertising source of your visit (for example, a UTM tag in the link).

Why we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, arrange and confirm a private visit or video walkthrough, and share information about this property. We do not sell, rent or share your details with third parties for marketing.

How it is sent and stored

Booking requests are delivered to the owner by email (through a form-delivery service) or by WhatsApp, depending on how the site is configured. They are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting sale discussion.

Cookies and analytics

We use privacy-friendly analytics only if you accept the cookie notice. If you decline, no analytics script is loaded. Your choice is remembered in your browser.
